## A Refined UI for Effortless Scorekeeping  

**BeloteScore** is a web app designed to **simplify score tracking** for **French Belote Bridgée**, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable gameplay experience. The interface is built for clarity and ease of use, allowing players to focus on their strategy rather than calculations.  

## A Thoughtful Balance of Design and Functionality  

From the **color scheme** to the **layout**, every detail was crafted to offer a **seamless user experience**. The design enhances readability and speed, making score entry quick and intuitive, even on mobile devices.  

## Fast, Accessible, and Built to Last  

Developed with **Next.js**, **React**, and **Tailwind CSS**, BeloteScore is optimized for **performance and accessibility**, ensuring a **responsive and lightweight** experience on any device.
