## A Complete Redesign for a Stunning Showcase  

This project was a **full revamp** of photographer **Arno Cauchois' website**, from design to development, with a strong focus on performance and SEO. More than just an update, it was a **ground-up transformation** to create a seamless and immersive experience.  

## Optimized for Aesthetics, Speed, and Visibility  

The new website balances **visual impact and efficiency**, ensuring Arno’s work takes center stage while maintaining fast loading times and an intuitive navigation experience across all devices. A deep **SEO optimization** strategy was implemented to boost organic reach and search rankings. The site also adapts to the user's system preferences, seamlessly switching between **light and dark modes** for an optimal browsing experience.  

## Attention to Every Detail, Including Legal Aspects  

Beyond the technical and visual aspects, I also handled the drafting of **legal notices and privacy policies**, ensuring full compliance with current regulations.
