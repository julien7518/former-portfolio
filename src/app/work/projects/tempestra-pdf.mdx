## A Seamless Blend of Design and Development  

**Tempestra** is a tool that allows users to **generate custom weather forecast PDFs** for Paris, with options for **portrait or landscape layouts**. Initially created to meet the needs of **West End Hotel**, this project combines **clean UX, efficient development, and a strong visual identity**.  

## A Thoughtful UI and Custom Branding  

Every design element, from the **logo** to the **PDF icons**, was crafted to create a polished and professional experience. The interface is sleek and intuitive, ensuring effortless customization and download.  

## Precision, Performance, and Practicality  

Built with **Next.js** and **Tailwind CSS**, Tempestra is optimized for **speed and accessibility**, making it a lightweight yet powerful tool for businesses needing up-to-date weather information in a branded format.
